# Synthetic Academic Regulations
These are development fixtures, not real university policies.

## 7.2 Attendance
Minimum attendance is 75% for the attendance condition of end-semester examinations.
This clause describes the attendance condition only; other exam requirements may apply.

## 8.1 Pass mark
Minimum pass mark is 40 out of 100. ABSENT and DETAINED are separate result states.

## 8.2 Supplementary examination procedure
Students with a failed course should submit the supplementary examination form to the examination office.
The examination office confirms the schedule and applicable fee. No fee amount is specified in this document.
